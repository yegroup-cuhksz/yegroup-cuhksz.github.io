## Education

- Sun Yat-sen University, PhD in physics (2025)

I am conducting research in the field of liquid xenon experiments, with a focus on neutrinos and dark matter. My work encompasses data and physics analysis, detector technology development, and the advancement of trace measurement systems. Our goal is to improve the detection sensitivity for both neutrino and dark matter, ultimately aiming to better understand the properties of neutrinos and make groundbreaking discoveries in dark matter. I welcome academic discussions and collaboration with undergraduate and PhD students.
