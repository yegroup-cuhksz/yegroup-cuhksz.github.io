- University of California, San Diego, PhD in physics (2020)

## Appointment

- The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, Tenure-track Assistant Professor, 2024 – present
- Columbia University, Postdoctoral research scientist, 2020 – 2023

Jingqiang Ye is the group’s principal investigator and a tenure-track assistant professor. His research focuses on neutrino physics and dark matter searches with noble-liquid detectors, as well as the development of low-radioactivity techniques. Outside the lab, he is a runner, coffee lover, and cat parent.
