## Platform

web

## Users

Four audiences, confirmed as equal priority:

- Prospective PhD students and postdocs deciding whether and how to apply.
- Researchers and academic collaborators checking the group's science, publications, and standing.
- Prospective undergraduates and other general visitors looking for an overview, recent activity, and contact details.
- Members and alumni looking for an accurate public record of the group, including photographs.

## Product Purpose

The official public website of the Neutrino & Dark Matter Group at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). It exists to:

- introduce the group and its scientific work;
- present research projects and results;
- show current members, their profiles, and former members;
- keep a current list of publications and representative papers;
- share group news, seminars, lectures, and photographs;
- tell prospective students and collaborators how to join or get in touch;
- establish a credible international academic presence.

Success means a visitor can quickly and truthfully understand what the group works on, who is in it, what it has published, what is happening, and how to make contact — and that the record stays current with modest maintenance effort.

## Positioning

The public face of one specific experimental particle-physics group working on dark matter and neutrino physics with noble-liquid detectors, chiefly through the XENONnT experiment at LNGS and the proposed RELICS reactor-neutrino experiment in Sanmen, China. Its authority comes from first-hand group material — the group's own members, publication record, activity records, and photographs — rather than generic academic-site copy. A neighboring group can copy the page structure but not the content.

## Operating Context

- The site is statically generated by Astro 6 from Markdown records and JSON data; it builds to `dist/` and is served by Nginx at `https://yegroup.cuhk.edu.cn`. No Node.js process, database, or CMS runs in production.
- Content is edited through structured records, not page templates: member Markdown in `src/content/members/`, research Markdown in `src/content/research/`, openings Markdown in `src/content/openings/`, and JSON in `src/data/` for publications, alumni, albums, news, activities, and site settings.
- Adding or changing a member, publication, project, news item, activity, or album updates every relevant page automatically; `scripts/validate-content.mjs` checks records, slugs, references, dates, image/CV paths, and editorial limits.
- Original photographs and the PI CV live under `public/assets/images/`; responsive WebP derivatives are generated into `public/media/` on every dev and production build.
- The site is a migration from the group's captured WordPress site. The legacy singular routes `/publication/` and `/opening/`, root-level member profile URLs such as `/jingqiang-ye/`, archived upload paths, and the WordPress query URLs `/?page_id=92` and `/?page_id=109` are preserved through explicit routing and redirects.
- The group's real rhythms include collaboration meetings, seminars, public lectures, new publications, group photographs, and periodic recruitment reviews. News and activities are added manually; no external feeds are collected automatically.
- Several editorial facts remain subject to group confirmation before public release; see **Evidence on Hand**.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Eight primary navigation sections: Home, Research, Activities, People, Openings, Publications, Gallery, Contact.
- Additional routes: Alumni, individual member profiles at the site root, a News archive with per-item detail pages, Gallery album pages, per-activity detail pages, a 404 page, sitemap, and robots file.
- The homepage order is: general introduction, Research, an open-positions band when roles are open, News, Activities, representative papers, Gallery. The original requirements listed News before Research; the implementation currently places Research first. Preview limits: News at most five (three by default), representative papers fixed at five, Gallery and Activities previews at three by default.
- An open-positions band appears after Research and before News when at least one role is open, showing the role names, the last-reviewed date, a link to Openings, and the PI email. It is omitted when no roles are open.
- Representative papers are an editorial selection stored with `featured` and `featured_order`, not an automatic "newest five" list. The Publications page is sorted newest first.
- News and Activities support `published` and `draft` states; drafts never appear on public pages, the homepage feed, or the sitemap.
- Research currently covers XENONnT and RELICS; the structure allows further projects. Member roles are free-form; missing optional fields are omitted rather than invented, and a missing portrait has an accessible fallback.
- Openings distinguishes open, closed, and general-inquiry states and displays a last-reviewed date; it must not imply a funded vacancy that does not exist.
- Constraints: static output only; no WordPress, PHP, database, or runtime framework; content updates must not require editing page templates; record references and local asset paths are validated at build time.
- Language: the site is English-only today, by decision. Chinese must be addable later without structural rework, so future work should avoid decisions that would force a rewrite when translation is introduced.
- Scientific terminology follows the experiments' own usage: WIMP dark matter, liquid xenon time projection chambers, CEvNS, XENONnT, RELICS, LNGS.

Open decisions:

- No content maintainer has been designated for News, Gallery, and recruitment review.
- The five representative papers are provisional pending group confirmation.
- Current recruitment availability and its last-reviewed date have not been freshly confirmed by the PI.

## Product Principles

1. **Truth over fill.** Never invent people, dates, milestones, vacancies, credits, or claims to complete a layout; show an honest empty or provisional state instead.
2. **Maintainers first.** Any member, publication, project, news item, activity, or album must be addable through content records alone, with every relevant page updating itself.
3. **Credibility is checkable.** Scientific claims, publication metadata, member details, and recruitment status must be traceable to real sources or explicit group confirmation.
4. **Addresses are durable.** Migration must preserve existing routes, media URLs, and external links so search results and citations keep working.
5. **Balance, not hierarchy.** Prospective applicants, academic peers, and public visitors matter equally; no one audience should crowd out another.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessible navigation, semantic HTML, keyboard-operable controls, and visible focus states are expected, and the project's acceptance record treats them as release checks. No formal conformance standard (such as a specific WCAG level) has been confirmed. Full-browser checks at 375, 768, and 1440 CSS px, 200% zoom, JavaScript-disabled use, and keyboard-only navigation remain outstanding.
