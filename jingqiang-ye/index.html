Jingqiang Ye is the group’s principal investigator and a tenure-track assistant professor. His research focuses on neutrino physics and dark matter searches with noble-liquid detectors, as well as the development of low-radioactivity techniques. His group is involved with the XENONnT dark matter experiment and RELICS reactor neutrino experiment, and he served as Analysis Coordinator for the XENONnT experiment from 2022 to 2023. Check out the full CV.

Outside the lab, he is a runner, coffee lover, and cat parent.
