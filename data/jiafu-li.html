My work encompasses data and physics analysis, detector technology development, and the advancement of trace measurement systems. Our goal is to improve the detection sensitivity for both neutrino and dark matter, ultimately aiming to better understand the properties of neutrinos and make groundbreaking discoveries in dark matter.
