We have open positions for Ph.D. students and Postdocs.

Unfortunately, we don’t have open positions for undergraduate students at the moment. We recommend checking back in the future or exploring other labs within the school.

PhD

Please email the following items to Prof. Jingqiang Ye (yejingqiang@cuhk.edu.cn) with a prefix of [CUHK-SZ PhD Application] in the title.

  • Transcript
  • CV
  • Research Statement (if any)

You can also find useful information on the university website: https://sse-mphil-phd.cuhk.edu.cn/en/program/PHY.

Postdoc

Please email the following items to Prof. Jingqiang Ye (yejingqiang@cuhk.edu.cn) with a prefix of [CUHK-SZ Postdoc Application] in the title.

  • CV (with publication list)
  • Research Statement


If you plan to formally apply for this position, please also include the following items.

  • Cover letter
  • 2 Recommendation Letters


If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to get in touch with us.
